Privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy describes how Lantern, a visual world for Discord communities, handles information. Contact the Lantern maintainer at emergent.worlds@proton.me with privacy questions or requests.

Information Lantern uses

  • Discord identity and membership: Discord user and server IDs, display name or server nickname, server name, membership status and whether you have permission to manage common spaces. During sign-in we request your identity, server list and membership in the selected server to verify access. We do not store your full server list.
  • World content: your character, appearance, home, rooms, links, geometry, scripts, object state, revision history and agent-building permissions.
  • Play state: room location, movement, seating, connection status and home routines. Your character can remain visible as an idle resident at home after you disconnect.
  • Conversation: messages and scripted speech delivered in your current room, including private object replies addressed to you.
  • Technical and support information: service authentication tokens, preview rate-limit records, and information you choose to email us. Hosting providers may process IP addresses, request metadata and diagnostic logs to deliver and protect the service.

Why we use it

We use this information to sign you in, verify community access, keep worlds persistent, synchronize play, enforce building permissions, render previews and respond to support requests. Lantern does not include advertising trackers or sell personal information.

Who can see it

People with access to the same Lantern community can see shared rooms, your character, home and visible world activity. Homes are shared spaces, not private storage. Room speech is delivered to players present in that room; private object replies are delivered only to the addressed player. Authorized property editors can inspect scripts and their persistent state. Do not put secrets or sensitive personal information into rooms, scripts, signs or chat.

Lantern uses Discord for authentication and Activity hosting, Cloudflare for web hosting, identity storage and preview rendering, and SpacetimeDB Maincloud for world data and synchronization. These providers process information needed to supply their services under their own policies. The maintainer may access stored data to operate the service, resolve problems and handle requests. Information may also be disclosed where legally required.

Coding agents

Connecting an agent grants the permissions you authorize in the Create tab. The agent can receive world data and publish changes within that scope. Your chosen agent and model provider may process data you share with them under their own policies. Lantern does not run model inference or collect your model-provider API keys. The downloaded MCP client stores its Lantern authentication token on your machine; you can revoke an agent's access in Create.

Storage and retention

Lantern stores a stable game identity and authentication token for each Discord member/server pair. Discord access tokens are used for sign-in and membership checks; the gateway does not persist Discord access or refresh tokens. The development browser client can store its game token in local storage. The public information pages use no cookies or analytics.

World content, identity mappings and membership records persist until removed; there is currently no automatic inactivity deletion schedule. Object source history retains up to 20 prior revisions. Room conversation retains at most 50 delivered messages per recipient, cleared on room changes, new connections or final disconnection. This does not erase screenshots or copies made by recipients, nor necessarily historical provider backups or logs.

Your choices and requests

You can stop using Lantern, revoke connected agents in Create and revoke Lantern's authorization in Discord settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete stored world data. To request access, correction or deletion, email emergent.worlds@proton.me with your Discord user ID and relevant server ID. We may ask for ownership verification; never send passwords, tokens or API keys. Requests are handled manually. We will explain any limitations, including shared content or records we must retain.

Eligibility and updates

Lantern is intended for people eligible to use Discord under Discord's minimum-age requirements in their country. If you believe an ineligible child's information was supplied, contact us. We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above.
